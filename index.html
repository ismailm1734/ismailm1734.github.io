Teams Meeting Assistant

Problem

Meeting follow-ups were manual: someone had to remember to write up a summary for participants, and answering "what did we decide on X" meant digging back through a transcript by hand.

What I did

I built an automated pipeline in n8n that pulls Microsoft Teams meeting transcripts, emails a summary to participants automatically, and answers follow-up questions about past meetings over WhatsApp. Supabase stores transcripts and summaries, and the Claude API handles summarization and the WhatsApp Q&A.

What I found

The hard parts were idempotency — making sure a transcript is never processed or sent twice — and carrying meeting access permissions through the pipeline correctly. It's now in active use at a 210-person logistics company.

Shoal

A 2D canvas game where you guide a school of fish with a light cursor instead of controlling them directly — the fish follow boids flocking rules (separation, alignment, cohesion) plus a weak attraction to the light. Predators hunt stray fish but flee from enough light intensity, so survival comes from staying grouped near the light rather than any single fish's skill.
