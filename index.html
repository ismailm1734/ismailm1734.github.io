01 · Case study

Tiny to Titan

3D evolution game with 2,400+ plays on Playgama, one of my 6 live games there.

I built a per-animal funnel from the event logs of the first 138 catalog players and found that half of the players who quit stopped at the 3rd to 5th animal, right where the XP needed jumped 5.5×. In v1.2.0 I flattened that curve, reworked the lake zone and tagged every event with its build version to compare releases.

02 · Case study

Wolf Arena

4v4 human-vs-werewolf shooter, live on Playgama, GameDistribution and CrazyGames.

Telemetry showed two problems in the first week. Almost every session started with the tutorial match again (194 tutorial starts in 188 sessions): a save bug on Playgama was resetting rank and gear each time. And about a quarter of players left before pressing Play. I fixed the save layer, made the tutorial match start on its own, and after looking at how comparable shooters onboard players (Krunker, Shell Shockers, Critical Ops, CoD Mobile), cut the tutorial from 9 steps to 4, with the rest taught as one-time hints the first time they are needed.

03 · Case study

Looped Mysteries

Instagram mystery brand and companion web game.

Each Reel is a "who is the ___?" mystery with five suspects, and the full case is playable on the web. I treat every Reel as an experiment, changing the next hook and setup based on reach, saves and follows, and the game tracks each case step by step from the first clue to the accusation. The account grew from under 700 to over 4,100 followers in its first week and to 6,900 by mid-November, and the first sales came from Instagram traffic.

What I built

A permission-scoped, multi-channel AI assistant system on n8n, Supabase and the WhatsApp Cloud API. About 20 automation flows are now in production. I mapped operational workflows with the company's AI specialist and turned them into testable automations.

Example: Teams Meeting Assistant
