01 · Case study

Tiny to Titan

3D evolution game with 811 plays on Playgama, one of my 3 live games there.

I built a per-animal funnel from the event logs of the first 138 catalog players and found that half of the players who quit stopped at the 3rd to 5th animal, right where the XP needed jumped 5.5×. In v1.2.0 I flattened that curve, reworked the lake zone and tagged every event with its build version to compare releases.

03 · Experience

AI Integration Intern, Öykü Lojistik

Jul – Sep 2026

What I built

A permission-scoped, multi-channel AI assistant system on n8n, Supabase and the WhatsApp Cloud API. About 20 automation flows are now in production. I mapped operational workflows with the company's AI specialist and turned them into testable automations.

Example: Teams Meeting Assistant

One of those flows pulls Microsoft Teams meeting transcripts, emails a summary to participants automatically, and answers follow-up questions about past meetings over WhatsApp. The hard parts were idempotency (a transcript is never processed or sent twice) and carrying meeting access permissions through the pipeline correctly.

04 · More games

Yuva Muhafızı

A third-person 3D action game where you play the colony's soldier ant. By day you raid a procedurally rearranged backyard for food and resin with your worker ants; by night you defend the queen against insect waves using telegraphed-attack combat, traps and caste evolutions. The game loop, AI, combat and UI are written from scratch on top of Three.js.

JavaScript · Three.js · Vite

Shoal

A 2D canvas game where you guide a school of fish with a light cursor instead of controlling them directly. The fish follow boids flocking rules plus a weak attraction to the light; predators hunt stray fish but flee from enough light, so survival comes from keeping the school together.

Cozy Pomodoro

A calm Pomodoro timer with a pixel-art companion who follows your sessions: she types while you focus, rests when you pause and walks to the couch on breaks. She is a chain of 8 AI-generated green-screen clips keyed live on a canvas. It also has a lofi radio, an always-on-top mini player and English and Turkish versions.
